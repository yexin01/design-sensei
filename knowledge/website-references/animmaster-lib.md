# Animmaster Lib (website reference)

## URL
https://animmasterlib.dev

## What it is
A commercial component library for animated web interfaces. The catalog classifies it as a website, reference-only, under proprietary paid terms.

## What was observed, and when
- 2026-10-09: the landing page and the terms page were read, and robots.txt allowed crawling.
- The terms grant a purchaser a non-exclusive licence and prohibit reselling or redistributing the library or its source files.
- The public category list is: scroll animations, mouse effects, page transitions, sliders, hero animations, WebGL shaders, text animations, 3D animations and more.
- Components are not freely available, so no component code exists in the local library and none was used or copied.

## Robots and terms stance
Although robots.txt permits crawling, the library's content is paid and licensed, so Design Sensei links to the site and records only the category names above. Nothing is mirrored or redistributed.

## How a person can use it legitimately
- Visit the site yourself, look at what the categories offer, and buy a licence if the library suits your project.
- Follow the licence you receive: use within your own project, no resale, no redistribution of the library or its files.
- Use the category list as a checklist when planning effects, then look for alternatives with open licences by searching the catalog for the same words. Related guidance: [kinetic typography](../design-styles/kinetic-typography.md), [motion-led storytelling](../design-styles/motion-led-storytelling.md), [aurora and gradient mesh](../design-styles/aurora-gradient-mesh.md), [experimental 3D](../design-styles/experimental-3d.md).
- Do not paste purchased component code into public repositories or into this catalog.

## Limits
- The catalog does not know the library's component names, APIs, dependencies, browser support or accessibility behaviour.
- Reduced-motion and fallback behaviour of its components is unverified; check it yourself before shipping.
- The terms may change; read the live terms page before buying.
