## Robots and terms stance
Design Sensei does not fetch, scrape, snapshot or redistribute any content from this site, and it respects the crawler restrictions. No file from the site exists in the local library, and no pattern or note may quote or paraphrase its pages. Because the terms were not reviewed, assume that reuse of any of its material requires reading and following those terms.

## How a person can use it legitimately
- Open the site in your own browser and browse the styles for inspiration, as any visitor would.
- Read its terms of use before copying, downloading or adapting anything, and follow them.
- If you adopt a style, record your own decisions (tokens, type, motion) in your project's own design file rather than pasting material from the site into this repository.
- Tell the assistant what you chose in your own words, so it can search the catalog for matching styles and patterns.
